OUR MISSION

Every wild animal deserves a life of freedom

Wildlife Paws SOS is a dedicated rescue, rehabilitation, and release centre based in the heart of Dullstroom, Mpumalanga. We are committed to being one of the most ethical wildlife centres in South Africa, where the animals always come first.

For the animals that cannot return to the wild, we provide a permanent, dignified home. We educate the public on why wild animals should never be kept as pets, and in collaboration with Dullstroom Bird of Prey, we offer an incredible diversity of species, from raptors and mammals to our beloved Vervet Monkeys.

PRIMARY FOCUS SPECIES

Vervet Monkeys, our heart and soul

Vervet Monkeys are at the very core of what we do. Highly social and incredibly intelligent, they are among the most frequent victims of the illegal pet trade and human-wildlife conflict in South Africa. We rescue, rehabilitate, and where possible, release them back into natural troops.

For those that can never return to the wild, we provide a permanent, dignified home, and use their stories to educate the public on why wild animals should never be kept as pets.
